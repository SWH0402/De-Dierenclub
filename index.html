About Me

About Me

Hi, I'm Stephanie.

I've spent years inside veterinary clinics as a veterinary technician, worked in animal health pharmaceuticals, and studied veterinary care formally. But the reason clients trust me with their home and their pet is simpler than that: I genuinely love animals, and it shows.

Handing over your key to someone you've just met takes real trust. I don't take that lightly. Every pet I care for gets treated as if they were my own, because in the time they're with me, they are.

I come to your home because research backs what most pet owners already feel: animals are calmer, eat better, and recover faster in their own environment. No kennels, no strangers, no disruption to their routine.

Based in Amsterdam Zuid, caring for pets across Amsterdam.
